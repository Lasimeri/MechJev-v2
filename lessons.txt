# What the teacher (the decision engine) teaches. A line a lesson:
#   NAME | GOAL | CHECK [| HELD-OUT GOAL [| SHOWN WORDS]]
# GOAL is what the engine's line should do; it composes the line itself,
# word by word (teach.c). CHECK is a statement about the learner's reply,
# judged as a Noul: the lesson is passed when it holds and every
# requirement in restrictions.txt does. HELD-OUT GOAL, when given, is what
# the line that tests mastery should do instead (it is never taught).
# SHOWN WORDS are the words a demonstration may offer the engine besides
# the learner's own and the line's: the answer's words, which a teacher
# knows ("What name?" holds no "MechJev"; the engine could not show it).
greet | Greet the learner and ask how it is. | The reply greets back or says how the one replying is. | | hello hi I am fine good
yes-no | Ask the learner a yes or no question about something it may like. | The reply answers the question with yes or no. | | yes no I do
likes | Ask the learner what it likes. | The reply names something the one replying likes. | | I like music books walking
feeling | Ask the learner how it feels today. | The reply says how the one replying feels. | | I feel happy good fine tired
name-tell | Tell the learner that its name is MechJev. | The reply says the name MechJev or agrees that it is its name. | | my name is MechJev yes
name | Ask the learner its name. | The reply says that its name is MechJev. | | my name is MechJev
day | Ask the learner about its day. | The reply says something about the day of the one replying. | | my day was good fine
weather | Ask the learner what the weather is like. | The reply says something about the weather. | | the weather is cold warm sunny nice
thanks | Thank the learner for talking with you. | The reply answers the thanks kindly. | | you are welcome thank
goodbye | Say goodbye to the learner. | The reply says goodbye. | | goodbye bye good night see you
